It's a new year. Perhaps that makes it time to reflect on the trials and tribulations of last year? Hmm. Not now, no. 

I'm back from about eighteen days of annual leave, and feeling fresh. In my role, there's going to be a lot of the same and then a few new challenges. The company will continue to grow. For me as a manager, that means more people to hire and be responsible for. 

My group's structure is reaching a bit of a limit, perhaps akin to the square-cube law. Four teams of four becomes five teams of five becomes six teams of six... eventually there's simply too many teams. The natural next step will be a new layer in the hierarchy, in the chain of management. Get some sub-management within a team. That'll be a big step - it'll be the first time I don't maintain a relationship of every single person within the structure. It'll only be sustainable to maintain relationships with two steps beneath me - my own reports, and their reports. 

But much is still the same - for all that talk of management, I remain incredibly eager to keep writing code. So part of the motivation of addressing the group's structure is to ensure that there's always time in the week for making changes to our repository.
