When does a year go as you expect it to? 

I talked about introducing layers of management - perhaps enough that I'd no longer be able to maintain a personal relationship with everyone in my group. It didn't quite go like that. We just about maintained our headcount. It was a slow first half for hiring, with other areas of the business being prioritised. We only got a small part of the attention of our recruiters. Then we stopped hiring. 

That didn't stop us from beginning to layer the responsibility a bit more. Our front-end team of mobile and web engineers is big enough now that we've made it into a little group of teams, where previous it was just a mega-team.
