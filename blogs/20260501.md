# Write once, read a million times

> Writing is an art.

The length of a piece needs to be justified by the topic, and the depth of context required to portray the one key message of the piece. 

This requires practice. I’m pretty bad at it.

## Vomiting documents

LLMs make it very easy to have a conversation, develop a load of context and conclusions, and have it vomit out a document. 

The current models seem to over-value the volume they output. In my opinion, they aren’t so good at evaluating a suitable length of output justified by the topic. 

## The effect on person-hours

So a colleague publishes a document for comment. Atlassian’s Confluence has a nice “time to read” feature: and you’ve been asked to read a fifteen minute document. 

Are you going to? 

You might actually be able to tell pretty quickly that it’s written by AI. When I see something written by AI, I know I devalue it in my head. 

I have a relationship with a human. I trust them, their skills, their output. If it's AI, am I certain it's not hallucinated? Am I certain it's had every part of its output scrutinised by its instructor?

If it’s not worth the effort of hand-crafting, why should you give it your full attention? 

Might you just skim it and try to jump to the conclusion? Or are you going to be tempted to load it into an LLM and say “summarise this”? 

Workplaces may be becoming more document-heavy now, since AI works so well when powered with a good depth of context.

But with documentation increasing, it’s likely LLMs themselves are writing a lot of it.  

## The slop

I fear that we’re barrelling towards a scenario where AI writes and AI reads, then human decides. And I fear that a model inflating a topic into a lengthy document, then a second model condensing it is only going to result in lower quality. 

Good decisions are made with the right context. 

There will be more to read than ever before, so we’ll get lazier. 

Finding something worth reading yourself will be harder than ever. Documents are written once and read many times over - a truth which has only increased now we’re all using LLMs. We need to optimise for reading. 

**So maybe it’s still worth writing yourself.**
