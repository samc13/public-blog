Today was a rather meaningful and fulfilling day in my career. Starling Bank had been invited by FinTech North as speakers at their conference in November 2024. 

We had a fifteen minute segment in a packed day, filled with a long list of locally based FinTech companies, including talks as well as fireside chats. 

Chloe Williams and I opted to focus on the 'Tech' side of FinTech - talking about Starling's approach to engineering and why it worked for us as a business. 

It was a particularly special day for me, as the conference was held in The University of Manchester's Whitworth Hall, a beautiful building that I had graduated from eight years prior. It really felt like the completing of a full circle. 

Chloe and I had gotten advice from a seasoned speaker within Starling, who had encouraged us to make bold or controversial statements; to capture interest; and to admit flaws so as to provide humility to the talk. 

We did that rather well - we told of how Starling had largely binned off JIRA (a ticket management system), and that Starling doesn't do much planning at all - we just focus on What's Now and What's Next. This was laced with confession that just because it worked for us, doesn't mean it will work for everyone. And just because it's good for us right now doesn't mean that it will be forever. 

This segment drew some raised eyebrows from the audience, and some friendly folk from Access Pay had quite a few questions for us after the talk was finished - they were eager to know more about life without JIRA. 

A final note: the guys at FinTech North have my gratitude and admiration. Rachel, Joe, Silas and Ryan are a small outfit but they pulled the entire day together flawlessly. It was a privilege to be invited along to speak, and a pleasure to be there for the day. 

---

A special day in a special venue. By no means the largest stage nor biggest crowd, but it felt significant to be talking on behalf of the company that I've loved working at for nearly five years.
